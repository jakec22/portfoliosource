Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password and enter your email. We'll send you a reset link — open it on your device and follow the prompts to set a new password.

How do I sign in?

You can sign in with your email and password, or use Sign in with Apple for a fast, private login.

How do I track my macros and calories?

From the Home tab, add foods to your daily log. You can reuse recent and favorite foods, build custom meals, and track your calories, protein, carbs, and fat against goals you set.

How do I set my goals?

Open the Profile tab to set your calorie and macro goals, water goal, and preferences. You can also use the goal wizard to get a recommended starting point.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

Reminders are managed in the app's settings. You can enable or disable meal and streak reminders and adjust the times. You can also turn notifications off entirely from your device's Settings → Notifications → Holy Macro.

How does the Apple Watch app work?

Start a workout from your iPhone and it opens on your Apple Watch automatically, showing live heart rate, calories, and elapsed time. You can check off sets and finish the workout from your wrist, and everything syncs back to your phone. Your heart-rate graph is saved with each workout.

The watch app requires an Apple Watch paired to your iPhone, with permission to read heart rate granted in the Health app.

Why isn't my heart rate showing on the watch?

Make sure the watch is snug on your wrist and that you've granted health permissions. If heart rate still doesn't appear, open the Health app on your iPhone and confirm Holy Macro has access to Heart Rate data.

How do I review my workout history?

Open the History tab (or the Exercise tab) to see past workouts, including sets, personal records, and heart-rate graphs.

How do I delete my account or my data?

You can delete your account from within the app's account settings. This deactivates your account and removes your information from our active databases. If you need help, email holymacroapp@gmail.com and we'll assist you.
